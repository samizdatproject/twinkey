Corner Shop (demo app)

An independent app that knows nothing about you. It lets you in with your Nostr key, then finds and checks your linked Bitcoin Cash address on Nostr relays, with no TwinKey server and no identity provider.

← Back to TwinKey (link your keys first)

Log in

  1. The shop issues a one-time login URL with a random nonce (valid 2 minutes, usable once).
  2. Your Nostr key signs it (NIP-98).
  3. The shop checks that signature, then looks up your NIP-39 list on relays and verifies the BCH link.
  4. Optional step-up: your BCH key signs the same nonce, proving you hold both keys right now.
